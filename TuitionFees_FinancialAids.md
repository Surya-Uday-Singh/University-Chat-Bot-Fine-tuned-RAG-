## 5.1 STUDENT ACCOUNTS OFFICE

Website: https://algomau.ca/students/student-accounts/

Email: accounts@algomau.ca

The Student Accounts Office is responsible for managing all matters related to student fees and financial accounts. This includes the assessment and collection of tuition and ancillary fees, processing payments, issuing receipts, and managing refunds and account adjustments. The office also provides support for student billing inquiries, financial holds, and payment plans. Students are encouraged to review their account statements regularly and contact the Student Accounts Office with any questions or concerns regarding their financial obligations to the University.

## Student's Responsibilities :

It is the responsibility of the student to:

- familiarize themselves with both the academic and financial regulations of Algoma University. This includes but is not limited to: payment of fees, schedule of fees, refund policies, registration/withdrawal policies, OSAP Deferral policies, Out of Province Fee Deferrals, and deadline dates.
- regularly view the status of their financial obligation. Algoma University is not required to mail or email Statement of Account in order to prompt payment. To determine account balances, students can access their Account Statements through the online student portal via students.algomau.ca.
- ensure that they allow sufficient time for payments to be received based on the method of payment (i.e. online bill payments may take up to five (5) business days before received by the University). Interest charges will not be waived for late payment.

International students and their dependents must enroll in the University Health Insurance Plan (UHIP), or a similar approved plan, for health coverage. Secondary students in their final year of studies may apply to Algoma U for admission on probation and if admitted, register concurrently in one 3 credit course per term. Those who wish to 'listen in' on courses at Algoma U must apply for admission. Tuition fees will be assessed at 50% of the fees quoted in this section of the Calendar. The International Tuition Exemption Policy outlines specific circumstances under which international students may be eligible to pay domestic tuition rates. ## 5.1.16 TUITION AND FEE REFUNDS

The amount of any refund (or reduction in outstanding fees) will be calculated according to the date by which the withdrawal request has been received. Deadlines are noted in the refund schedule posted below. It is important to note that withdrawal deadlines and academic penalty deadlines are not necessarily the same.

## General Refund Policy

Students wishing to withdraw from courses or programs must do so through the Office of the Registrar. When the student is withdrawn, they may be eligible for any applicable refund of tuition fees (or reduction of indebtedness to the University). Failure to formally withdraw by the refund deadlines outlined below will result in the student being responsible for their full assessment of fees. Calculation of a refund will be based on the number of credits from which the student is withdrawing as well as the length of time that the student has been registered in the term. No refunds are given for withdrawals after the last date to withdraw, as outlined within the Tuition Fee Refund Schedule. Refunds are assessed after the final day to drop and receive a refund for each term.

## Tuition Deposit Refunds

## Domestic Students:

Tuition deposits are non-refundable. Students who withdraw on or before the first day of classes are eligible for a full refund of tuition and fees less the non-refundable registration deposit.

## International Students:

International students, in their first or second term of study, should refer to our International Student Refund Policy for refund eligibility. All refunds requests MUST be submitted via the International Student Withdrawal and Refund Request Form.

If an International Student in their third term or higher, officially withdraws on or before the 14th day of the term, the tuition paid will be credited to your student account, less the $1,000 non-refundable tuition deposit. The registration fee and ancillary fees are non-refundable after the first day of class and will not be carried to a subsequent term. Tuition is non-refundable after the 14th day of the term.

Processing times for refunds vary, but Students can expect a minimum of 8-10 (eight to ten) weeks from the date Algoma University receives the request and ALL required documents.

## Additional Refund Information

- Tuition deposits are non-refundable.
- Ancillary fees are non-refundable after the first day of class.
- Tuition is non-refundable after the 14th day of the term noted in the Tuition Fee Refund Schedule.
- Domestic Refunds are issued by electronic money transfer via PayMyTuition. Please allow a minimum of two weeks to process.
- International Refunds are issued via PayMyTuition. Please allow a minimum of 8-10 (eight to ten) weeks to process from the time we receive all required documents.
- Students who receive OSAP funding may have any tuition/ancillary fee refund returned to the National Student Loans Service Centre to put toward the balance of their loans/grants.

## 5.2.6 GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP AND AWARD REGULATIONS AND GUIDELINES

Undergraduate Admissions Scholarship Programs

## Algoma University Awards of Excellence

Awards of Excellence scholarships are available to students entering a degree program at Algoma University with an 80% that have completed all their qualifying requirements in the academic year prior to commencing university studies at Algoma University. The offer and adjudication process for Awards of Excellence is available on our website.

Platinum $16,000 ($4000 annually) to all candidates presenting an entrance average of 95% or greater.

Gold $12,000 ($3000 annually) to all candidates presenting an entrance average of 90-94.9%

Silver $10,000 ($2500 annually) to all candidates presenting an entrance average of 85-89.9%.

Bronze $6000 ($1500 annually) all candidates presenting an entrance average of 80-84.9%.

Awards of Excellence are not available to international students. To hold and renew an Award of Excellence, eligible candidates must abide by the terms and conditions of their award. Renewal of the award is based on the academic average of all courses taken in the previous year (fall/winter), including any failures. The value of the renewal will be determined based on the student's academic average during their previous year of studies. Students that fail to meet all requirements to hold/renew their award will permanently lose eligibility for this award. Awards of Excellence are not eligible for deferral.

International Admissions Awards are available to eligible students entering with a minimum average of 80% that have completed all of their qualifying requirements in the academic year prior to commencing studies in a degree program at Algoma University. Under no circumstances will scholarships be deferred. Students must accept the terms and conditions of admission and the scholarship offered. As awards require enrolment over two semesters within an academic year (fall/winter), students entering in winter or spring will be considered for their award in the next academic year. The offer and adjudication process is available on our website.

Chancellors $20,000 ($5000 annually) to candidates entering with an A+

Presidents $2500 to candidates entering with an A

Dean's $1000 to candidates entering with a B+

Award of Excellence $500 to candidates entering with a B

To hold an International Admissions Award, candidates must maintain enrolment in a minimum of 24 credits over two consecutive terms of study (fall/winter). For the Chancellor's award, candidates must maintain enrolment in 30 credits over two consecutive terms of study (fall/winter) in their first year of study and must maintain an overall average of 80% on all courses over fall/winter, including any failures.

Continuing scholarships, bursaries and awards are available to students entering their second, third, or fourth year of an undergraduate program. We offer awards with varying criteria - some requiring an application. Application-based awards are considered through the Entrance/Continuing Awards Application. The deadline to submit this application is May 15 for first consideration, with a final deadline of June 30. The application and information is available through the Self-Service Portal.

Algoma University Achievement Awards - $1,000 for students who have maintained a minimum average of 80% on 30 credits taken in two consecutive terms of study in the previous fall/winter academic year at Algoma University. Eligible candidates must enrol in and maintain 30 credits over two consecutive terms (fall/winter) of study in the year they hold the award. This award is non-cumulative with any renewable admission award, including named awards. Students will be considered for this award each year of their undergraduate degree.

## 5.2.8 AWARD TENURE AND RENEWAL

## Changes in Registration Status

Students who receive financial aid-such as awards, scholarships, government loans, or grants-and then reduce their course load below the required level may lose some or all of their funding, which can result in academic, financial, or other consequences. Students are responsible for notifying the Financial Aid and Awards Office in writing of any changes to their registration status and may also need to inform their provincial or territorial student loan authority. The university may report these changes to the appropriate lending agency. To avoid unexpected financial impacts, students are strongly encouraged to consult with the Financial Aid and Awards Office before making any changes to their registration.

## Scholarship Renewal

Students receiving awards that are eligible for renewal must meet all the conditions specified for renewal consideration. These requirements vary for each award and can be found on our website. It is the student's responsibility to review and understand the specific renewal criteria for their award.

## Scholarships and Co-op Programs

For students in co-operative education programs, consult with the Financial Aid &amp; Awards Office regarding financial aid and awards.

## Students with Disabilities

Algoma University strives to ensure that students with disabilities have equal access to awards. Students with a documented disability can request consideration for receipt and renewal of awards at a reduced course load. Consideration of eligibility will only be given to students who have identified their disability with the Learning Centre at Algoma University as of the first day of classes and where accommodations require a reduced course load. In all cases, professional documentation will be necessary to outline the nature of the disability.

## Ineligibility for Scholarship and Awards

Students who are found to be in violation of the Student Code of Conduct, Academic and the Student Code of Conduct, Non-Academic will be deemed ineligible and all university scholarships revoked for all subsequent terms. If the violation occurs prior to the awarding of the scholarship monies, the student will forfeit the award for the term. If violation occurs after the awarding of the scholarship for the current term, the university will not seek restitution, however, in both instances, the scholarship will be terminated for all subsequent terms.

For more information on financial aid, scholarships, bursaries and/or awards, please visit our website.
